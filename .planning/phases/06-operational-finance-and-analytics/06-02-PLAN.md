# 06-02-PLAN: Operational Risk Register, IAC Compliance Checklists & Legal Documents

## Objective
Implement compliance and audit-readiness infrastructure: Operational Risk Register (5×5 severity matrix), Internal Audit Committee (IAC) compliance checklists, and the secure legal document repository.

## Proposed Changes
1. **Database Schema Additions**:
   - `risk_register_entries`: Risk category (`FINANCIAL`, `LEGAL`, `HEALTH_SAFETY`, `PARTNER_RELATIONS`, `REPUTATION`), probability (1-5), impact (1-5), severity score (`probability * impact`), mitigation plan, status (`IDENTIFIED`, `MITIGATING`, `CLOSED`).
   - `iac_audit_items`: Mandatory audit requirements (Signed Contract, MOU, Insurance, Host Verification, Financial Receipts, Standards Vault), compliance status (`PENDING_AUDIT`, `VERIFIED`, `FLAGGED_NON_COMPLIANT`), audited by, audit notes.
   - `legal_documents`: Category (`CONTRACT`, `AGREEMENT_MOU`, `INSURANCE`, `VISA_LETTER`, `EMERGENCY_CONTACT_FORM`), validity dates, expiration warnings, document reference.

2. **FastAPI Backend (`services/api/app/modules/legalities/`)**:
   - `models.py`: SQLAlchemy models for risks, IAC audit items, and legal documents.
   - `service.py`: Risk severity calculations, IAC compliance progress evaluation, expiration alerts.
   - `router.py`: REST endpoints at `/api/v1/legalities/`.

3. **Automated Verification**:
   - Pytest tests in `services/api/tests/test_legalities_compliance.py` verifying risk matrix calculations, IAC compliance checks, and document expiration detection.
