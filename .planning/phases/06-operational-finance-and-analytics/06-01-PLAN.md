# 06-01-PLAN: Financial Pricing Engine, Payment Milestone Tracking & Approval Queue

## Objective
Implement deterministic financial pricing, payment milestone tracking, and a human-gated approval queue for fee waivers and refunds in accordance with Section 3.9 of the Master Specification.

## Proposed Changes
1. **Database Schema (`database/migrations/006_finance_and_analytics_schema.sql`)**:
   - `pricing_rule_sets`: versioned rulesets for iGV, iGTa, and iGTe (base fee, duration tiers, weekly rates, currency MAD/EUR/USD).
   - `pricing_calculations`: immutable history of calculations evaluated for opportunities/contracts.
   - `payment_obligations`: tranches, expected amount, due date, status (`PENDING`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `PAID`, `OVERDUE`, `WAIVED`).
   - `payments`: transaction references, receipts, payment dates, payer details.
   - `refunds` & `financial_cases`: case type (`FEE_WAIVER`, `DISCOUNT_REQUEST`, `REFUND`, `ANNEX_2_PENALTY`), amount, reason, status (`PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`), reviewed by, audit trail.

2. **FastAPI Backend (`services/api/app/modules/finance/`)**:
   - `models.py`: SQLAlchemy models for pricing rule sets, calculations, obligations, payments, and financial cases.
   - `service.py`:
     - Deterministic calculation function: `calculate_pricing(rule_set_id, duration_weeks, modality, num_trainees)`.
     - Generation of payment obligations from contract terms (e.g. 30% on Match, 70% on Realize).
     - Payment recording and receipt attachment.
     - Financial approval workflow: role-gated to `LCP_ADMIN`, `LCVP_FINANCE`, or `LCVP_ICX`. Strictly blocks autonomous AI approvals.
   - `router.py`: REST endpoints at `/api/v1/finance/`.

3. **Automated Verification**:
   - Pytest tests in `services/api/tests/test_finance_pricing.py` verifying pricing formulas, overdue calculations, payment milestone tracking, and waiver approval permissions.
