# Plan 10-02: Interactive Visual Components & Domain Graphs Engine

## Objective
Implement the 7 specialized graph archetypes specified in Section 4 of `Aiesec Icx Erp Dashboard Kpis And Graph Architecture.md`, along with the micro-visual previews for the collapsed cards.

## Execution Skills
- `ui-styling`
- `frontend-design`
- `ui-ux-pro-max`
- `animate`

## Detailed Tasks
1. Build reusable, high-efficiency graph components in `src/components/dashboard/graphs/`:
   - `SteppedFunnelChart.tsx`: Horizontal stepped funnel with conversion rate bridges (`CR%`) between stages (`APP` → `SHORTLISTED` → `ACC` → `APD` → `RE` → `FIN` → `CO`) with direct data labels.
   - `BulletBarChart.tsx`: Horizontal bullet chart with solid actual bar, target line, and RAG background zones (for SLAs and process velocity).
   - `HeatmapMatrix.tsx`: 2D color-coded matrix (for 18 Exchange Standards × products/cohorts, and member meeting attendance across weeks W1..W16).
   - `QuadrantScatterPlot.tsx`: 2×2 decision quadrant matrix with labeled quadrants, interactive bubbles, and direct tooltips (for IR Partner strategic quadrant and Member Engagement vs. Performance).
   - `StackedHorizontalBar.tsx`: 100% and absolute stacked horizontal bars with direct percentage and count badges (for slot capacity, pipeline sector breakdown, and 4-tier member distribution).
   - `PaceLineChart.tsx`: Dual-line S-curve pace chart comparing solid actual trajectory against dashed target curve across weeks W1..W26.
   - `FinancialWaterfallChart.tsx`: Cashflow waterfall chart showing revenue additions, cost step-downs, and final net surplus in MAD with AFPM sustainability ratio badge.
2. Build Collapsed Card Micro-Visuals in `src/components/dashboard/graphs/micro-visuals.tsx`:
   - Mini Horizontal Funnel (Card 1)
   - Mini Pipeline Bar (Card 2)
   - Mini Capacity Bar (Card 3)
   - Mini Top 5 Countries Bar (Card 4)
   - 14-Day Lock Readiness Strip (Card 5)
   - 18-Block Mini Standards Strip (Card 6)
   - Bed Occupancy Bullet Bar (Card 7)
   - AFPM Sustainability Ratio Bar (Card 8)
   - 4-Tier Member Distribution Bar (Card 9)
3. Ensure strict adherence to Universal Color & Typography Encoding Rules:
   - Emerald `#10B981` (On Track / Tier 1)
   - AIESEC Blue `#037EF3` (Active / Tier 2)
   - Amber `#F59E0B` (Warning / Tier 3)
   - Rose Red `#EF4444` (Breach / Alert / Tier 4)
   - Direct visible data labels on every element; responsive and zero-clutter.
