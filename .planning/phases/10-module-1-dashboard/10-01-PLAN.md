# Plan 10-01: Dashboard Domain Data Engine & KPI Calculation Core

## Objective
Establish the foundational data structures, mathematical calculation formulas, RAG thresholds, and domain datasets for the 9 Clickable Dashboard Cards defined in `Aiesec Icx Erp Dashboard Kpis And Graph Architecture.md`.

## Execution Skills
- `design-system`
- `vercel-composition-patterns`

## Detailed Tasks
1. Create `src/lib/dashboard/dashboard-types.ts`:
   - Filter types: `TermFilter` (`'26.1' | '26.2' | 'CUSTOM' | 'PEAK_WINTER' | 'PEAK_SUMMER'`), `ProductFilter` (`'ALL' | 'iGV' | 'iGTa' | 'iGTe'`), `EngineFilter` (`'ALL' | 'EXTERNAL' | 'INTERNAL'`), `RoleFilter` (`'ALL' | 'LCVP' | 'MM_TL' | 'TM'`).
   - Domain Card IDs: `card-1` (Exchange Funnel), `card-2` (B2B CRM), `card-3` (Opportunities/Slots), `card-4` (IR Supply), `card-5` (Pre-Arrival/14d Lock), `card-6` (CXP/18 Standards), `card-7` (Accommodation/Pickup), `card-8` (AFPM Finance), `card-9` (Member Engagement & TeSt).
   - KPI definition model: `id`, `name`, `type` (`MoS` | `KPI`), `formula`, `sourceTable`, `target`, `unit`, `ragThresholds` (`green`, `amber`, `red`), `responsibleRole`, `currentValue`, `wowDelta`, `achievedPercentage`.
   - Card model: `id`, `title`, `domain`, `healthBadge` (`GREEN` | `AMBER` | `RED`), `primaryMos`: `{ label: string, value: string, target: string, pct: number }`, `headlineKpis`: `Array<{ label: string, value: string, wowDelta: string, status: 'positive' | 'negative' | 'neutral' }>`, `microVisualType`: `'funnel' | 'stacked_bar' | 'capacity_bar' | 'countries_bar' | 'readiness_strip' | 'standards_strip' | 'occupancy_bullet' | 'breakeven_bullet' | 'engagement_bar'`.
   - Member engagement types: 4 Tiers (`TIER_1_CORE`, `TIER_2_STRONG`, `TIER_3_MODERATE`, `TIER_4_DROPOFF`), Composite MES formula, Rabat 6 Leadership Routines, O2O logs, and Team Standards (`BUILDING`, `PERFORMING`, `CLOSING`).
2. Create `src/lib/dashboard/dashboard-data-engine.ts`:
   - Pure mathematical calculations for every KPI across the 9 domains.
   - Filtering function that computes domain card values and KPI scorecards dynamically based on active filter state.
   - Comprehensive mock datasets adhering to AIESEC in Rabat records (`Rayen Jbali`, `Firdawsse Jray`, `Abdenasser Moussafi`, `BMCE Bank Foundation`, `Rabat American School`, etc.).
3. Write automated tests in `tests/dashboard-data-engine.test.ts` verifying all formulas and filter computations.
