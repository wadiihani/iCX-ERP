# Plan 02-01: Exchange Standards Parallel-Truth Service (M2)

## Objective
Implement the Exchange Standards parallel-truth data service managing the 16 statutory standards (#1–#16), strictly maintaining two independent sources of truth: AIESEC operational tracking (`tracked_status`, `proof_id`, `tracked_by`) and the participant's immutable voice (`ep_reported_answer`, `ep_reported_at`), enforcing INV-05 and DQ-055 mismatch detection.

## User Persona & Role
Standards & Quality Lead & Customer Experience Analyst

## Specialized Skills
`supabase-postgres-best-practices`, `emil-design-eng`, `ui-styling`

## Scope
1. **Parallel-Truth Tracking Service (`src/lib/data-integrity/standards-service.ts`)**:
   - `updateTrackedStatus()`: Updates tracking state (`not_started`, `in_progress`, `delivered`, `not_delivered`, `n_a`), records `tracked_at`, `tracked_by`, and links optional `proof_id`.
   - `recordEpSurveyResponse()`: Records the participant's survey response. Strictly enforces write-once immutability via `assertEpReportedImmutable` (INV-05).
   - Delivery classification: Flags standards marked `delivered` without a `proof_id` as `delivered_unproven` (§6.4).
2. **Computed Quality Metrics (§6.3)**:
   - `std_delivery_pct`: Delivered mandatory host standards ÷ total mandatory host standards (Standards #4, #5, #7, #8, #10, #11, #13, #14).
   - `completion_readiness`: `true` only if all 8 mandatory host standards are `delivered` AND have verified `proof_id`s attached.
   - Host standards vs Home standards separation (#1–#3, #16 delivered by Home Entity; #4–#15 delivered by Host Entity).
3. **DQ-055 Mismatch Detector (§6.3, §13.2)**:
   - Detects discrepancies where AIESEC marked `delivered` but the EP answered negatively ("No", "Changed, not agreed", "Did not attend"), or vice versa.
   - Raises structured `DQ-055` Review issues for human investigation (review only; never auto-edits EP response).

## Verification
- Comprehensive test suite in `tests/exchange-standards-service.test.ts` verifying:
  - Parallel tracking preserving independent truths.
  - INV-05 write-once rejection when EP survey answer is altered.
  - Calculation of `std_delivery_pct` and `completion_readiness`.
  - Amber `delivered_unproven` flag when `proof_id` is omitted.
  - DQ-055 issue detection when EP answers conflict with tracked delivery.
