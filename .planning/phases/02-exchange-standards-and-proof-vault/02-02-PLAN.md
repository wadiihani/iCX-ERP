# Plan 02-02: Standards Checklist Validation Engine & Computed Metrics (M2)

## Objective
Implement the statutory checklist item validation engine for Exchange Standards #4 through #15 according to §6.2, guaranteeing that an operational standard cannot be marked `delivered` unless all constituent statutory delivery criteria are met, and provide co-delivery tracking for Home Entity standards (#1, #2, #3, #16 per §6.4).

## User Persona & Role
Standards & Quality Lead & Customer Experience Analyst

## Specialized Skills
`supabase-postgres-best-practices`, `emil-design-eng`, `ui-styling`

## Scope
1. **Statutory Delivery Checklist Definitions (§6.2)**:
   - Specific, granular verification checklist items for all Host Entity standards (#4 through #15):
     - Standard 4 (Expectations setting): 6 statutory checklist items (culture, safety, political situation, living standards, contacts, accommodation conditions).
     - Standard 5 (Visa & work permit): invitation letter, supporting docs (or visa not required).
     - Standard 6 (Arrival pickup): pickup point agreed pre-travel, domestic transport info sent, AIESEC rep pickup done.
     - Standard 7 (Accommodation): conditions matched, single bed, clean water, bathroom, electricity.
     - Standard 8 (Incoming prep): seminar before work starts, personal goals follow-up, culture, living standards.
     - Standard 9 (First day of work): rep accompanies EP to workplace.
     - Standard 10 (Alignment spaces): alignment space at start (JD ↔ goals) and debrief space at end.
     - Standard 11 (Job description): executed matches posted, changes communicated & agreed.
     - Standard 12 (Working hours): hours match posted, hours within rule.
     - Standard 13 (Duration): duration matches posted, duration within programme duration rule.
     - Standard 14 (Opportunity benefits): all posted benefits delivered.
     - Standard 15 (Departure support): written departure procedure sent ≥ 14 days before end.
2. **Checklist Validation & Enforcement Engine (`src/lib/data-integrity/standards-checklist.ts`)**:
   - `getStandardChecklist(exchangeId, stdNo)`: Retrieves checklist items and their completion status.
   - `updateChecklistItem(exchangeId, stdNo, itemKey, completed, actorId)`: Updates individual checklist item with audit trail.
   - `validateChecklistCompleteness(exchangeId, stdNo)`: Validates that all items are checked before marking `delivered`. Rejects invalid transitions with clear error and missing item list.
   - `getExchangeChecklistProgress(exchangeId)`: Calculates granular checklist completeness across all 12 host standards.
3. **Co-delivery Alignment Tracker (§6.4)**:
   - Tracks alignment for Home Entity standards (#1, #2, #3, #16) per IR partner: delivery method, tracking method, audit evidence, reporting routine.
4. **Integration with `standardsService`**:
   - `standardsService.updateTrackedStatus` enforces checklist completeness when transitioning to `delivered`.

## Verification
- Comprehensive test suite in `tests/standards-checklist.test.ts` verifying:
  - Retrieval and updating of statutory checklist items for standards #4–#15.
  - Rejection of `delivered` status transition when checklist items are incomplete.
  - Successful transition to `delivered` when all checklist items are fulfilled.
  - Co-delivery tracking for Home Entity standards (#1, #2, #3, #16).
  - Audit logging of all checklist item state changes (INV-09).
