# Plan 05-01: Exchange Lifecycle Records, Modality Checklists & Incident Workflows

## Objective
Implement operational Exchange lifecycle records spawned upon approved matches, modality-specific pre-arrival checklists (`PHYSICAL` logistics vs `REMOTE` digital setup), Incoming Preparation Seminar (IPS) tracking, worksite readiness, and formal exception/incident workflows (early finish, late finish, break requests, emergency escalations).

## Scope
1. **Database Schema & Migrations**:
   - `exchanges` table: UUID, application_id, exchange_code, modality (`PHYSICAL`, `PARTLY_REMOTE`, `FULLY_REMOTE`), delivery_status (`PRE_ARRIVAL`, `ARRIVED`, `REALIZED`, `FINISHED`, `COMPLETED`, `EARLY_FINISH`, `BROKEN`), start_date, end_date, actual_arrival_date, actual_departure_date, ips_completed, worksite_verified, first_day_attended, lead_manager_id, notes.
   - `exchange_checklists` table: UUID, exchange_id, category (`PRE_ARRIVAL`, `ARRIVAL`, `IPS`, `WORKSITE`, `LEGAL`, `DEPARTURE`), item_key, item_title, is_mandatory, is_completed, completed_at, completed_by, notes.
   - `exchange_incidents` table: UUID, exchange_id, incident_type (`HEALTH`, `SAFETY`, `HARASSMENT`, `LEGAL`, `CONTRACT_BREACH`, `BREAK_REQUEST`), severity (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`), description, reported_by, status (`OPEN`, `INVESTIGATING`, `ESCALATED`, `RESOLVED`), resolution_notes, resolved_at.
2. **Domain Models & Pre-Arrival Checklist Generator**:
   - Auto-generates modality-specific tasks upon exchange creation:
     - `PHYSICAL`: Visa confirmation, flight ticket upload, emergency contact verification, health insurance copy, airport pickup assigned, accommodation safety checked, IPS invitation.
     - `REMOTE`: Virtual tool access, remote contract signed, time-zone alignment agreement, virtual IPS.
3. **Exception & Incident Escalation Engine**:
   - Incident reporting with severity classification and audit history.
   - Break request workflow: records justification, impacts, and requires formal human approval before altering delivery dates or statuses.
4. **REST Endpoints**:
   - `GET /api/v1/exchanges`: list operational exchanges with delivery status and modality filters.
   - `POST /api/v1/exchanges/spawn-from-app/{app_id}`: spawn exchange record from approved application.
   - `GET /api/v1/exchanges/{id}`: get exchange details with checklists and incident history.
   - `PUT /api/v1/exchanges/{id}/checklists/{item_id}`: toggle checklist completion with actor logging.
   - `POST /api/v1/exchanges/{id}/incidents`: report operational incident or break request.
   - `PUT /api/v1/exchanges/{id}/incidents/{incident_id}/resolve`: resolve incident with formal audit notes.

## Dependencies
- Phase 4 Application & Match approval engine.
