# Plan 05-02: Arrival Logistics & Accommodation Inventory Boards

## Objective
Build the arrival logistics management board (flight tracking, airport/station pickups, transport assignments) and the accommodation inventory management system (host assignments, apartment safety inspections, check-in/check-out dates) ensuring physical exchange participants in Rabat are hosted safely in full compliance with local regulations and AIESEC housing standards.

## Scope
1. **Database Schema & Migrations**:
   - `arrival_logistics` table: UUID, exchange_id, flight_number, airline, arrival_airport (`RBA_RABAT`, `CMN_CASABLANCA`, `RAK_MARRAKECH`), arrival_datetime, terminal, pickup_status (`PENDING`, `ASSIGNED`, `EN_ROUTE`, `PICKED_UP`, `NO_SHOW`), assigned_driver_id, transport_mode (`CAR`, `TRAIN`, `TAXI`), driver_contact, notes.
   - `accommodation_units` table: UUID, unit_name, address, neighborhood (`Agdal`, `Hassan`, `Hay_Riad`, `Medina`, `Ocean`), unit_type (`HOST_FAMILY`, `AIESEC_APARTMENT`, `DORMITORY`, `HOTEL`), capacity_beds, occupied_beds, monthly_rent_mad, landlord_name, landlord_phone, safety_inspection_passed, last_inspected_at.
   - `exchange_accommodations` table: UUID, exchange_id, accommodation_unit_id, check_in_date, check_out_date, status (`PLANNED`, `CHECKED_IN`, `CHECKED_OUT`), bed_number, key_deposit_mad, notes.
2. **Safety & Capacity Enforcement**:
   - Accommodation safety checklist: locks, fire safety, basic sanitation, hot water, heating/ventilation verified prior to check-in.
   - Over-capacity check: prevents assigning an EP to an accommodation unit where `occupied_beds >= capacity_beds`.
3. **REST Endpoints**:
   - `GET /api/v1/logistics/arrivals`: list upcoming arrivals with pickup statuses.
   - `POST /api/v1/logistics/arrivals`: record or update flight arrival details.
   - `PUT /api/v1/logistics/arrivals/{id}/status`: update pickup status (e.g. `PICKED_UP`).
   - `GET /api/v1/accommodations`: list housing units with capacity and safety status.
   - `POST /api/v1/accommodations`: register housing unit.
   - `POST /api/v1/accommodations/assign`: assign EP to housing unit with capacity validation.

## Dependencies
- Phase 5-01 Exchange domain models.
