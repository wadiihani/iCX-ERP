## Detailed Tasks
1. Upgrade Case Solving Vault in `src/app/admin/cases/` and `src/lib/cases/quality-case-engine.ts`:
   - Separate Corporate CSR Sponsors & Institutions into dedicated CRM view (`/crm/sponsors`).
   - On Quality Case records, display live statutory countdown badges:
     - **Stage 1 (Local Resolution)**: `24h Ack / 48h Reply / 72h Escalate`
     - **Stage 2 (ECB National)**: `48h Ack / 96h Decision`
     - **Stage 3 (ICB Global)**: `48h Ack / 5d Final Verdict`
     - **Annex 2 Replacement Timer**: `14d APD / 7d RE` countdown.
     - Confidentiality mask on `HARASSMENT_COMMITTEE_ANNEX1` cases (`24h start / 7 working days report`).
2. Integrate Annex 2 Financial Liability Link:
   - In `runAnnex2Calculator()` (`src/lib/cases/compensation-calculator.ts`): add `+ Save Calculation as Case Claim & AFPM Liability Reserve` button.
   - Saves computed liability in MAD directly into the linked Case and creates a provision in the AFPM Finance ledger.
3. Completely Separate Finance from Team Members:
   - Eliminate `db.financeAndTeam`. Finance transactions strictly live in `db.transactions`.
4. Build Invoices & Accounts Receivable Table (`db.invoices`) in `src/app/dashboards/page.tsx`:
   - Columns: `Invoice #`, `Partner / Sponsor / EP`, `Product`, `Total TTC (MAD)`, `Paid (MAD)`, `Balance Due (MAD)`, `Issue Date`, `Due Date`.
   - Automatic RAG status badges with prominent **`OVERDUE >14 DAYS (5% Penalty Warning & Realization Freeze)`** per AFPM Sec. 6 and APIP 3.2.5.
   - iGV Partner / CSR Cost Coverage Gauge (`≥70–100% target`).
