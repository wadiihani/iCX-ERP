# Plan 16-02: Dedicated Accommodation Bed Allocation Board & Airport Pickup Logistics Tracker

## Objective
Implement Section 7 (`CHG-34`, `CHG-35`) of `aiesec_icx_erp_vercel_audit_and_complete_changes_blueprint.md`: split housing and pickups into two independent database schemas and operational views, enforce 4 physical vetting checks with bed allocation, and track airport pickups with buddy assignments and Standard #6 guide verification.

## Execution Skills
- `supabase-postgres-best-practices`
- `impeccable`
- `mobile-native`

## Detailed Tasks
1. Split Logistics into Two Dedicated Schemas in `src/lib/types/accommodation.ts` & `src/lib/types/arrival-readiness.ts`:
   - **`db.accommodations` (`Standard #7`)**:
     - `id`, `providerName`, `providerType` (`HOST_FAMILY`, `HOSTEL`, `FLAT`, `DORM`), `neighborhood`, `hostContactPhone`, `hostContactEmail`, `address`.
     - 4 Mandatory Physical Vetting Checkboxes: `singleBedProvided`, `runningWaterVerified`, `bathroomOperational`, `electricityVerified`.
     - `inspectionPhotoUrl`, `inspectionDate`, `inspectorUserId`.
     - `beds`: array of `{ bedId, roomLabel, genderRule, costPerMonthMad, depositMad, assignedEpId, checkInDate, checkOutDate, depositReturned }`.
     - Mathematical exclusion preventing double-booking of any `bedId` across overlapping dates.
   - **`db.pickups` (`Standard #6`)**:
     - `id`, `epId`, `arrivalPort` (`CMN`, `RBA`, `Rabat Agdal`, `Rabat Ville`, `Tangier`), `flightOrTrainNumber`, `arrivalTimestamp`, `assignedBuddyUserId`, `writtenGuideSent3dPrior` (`boolean`), `simCardActivated` (`boolean`), `madExchanged` (`boolean`), `completedTimestamp`, `notes`.
2. Update Accommodation Board in `src/app/accommodation/page.tsx`:
   - Tab 1: **Accommodation Providers & Bed Matrix** with actions: *Inspect/Edit Provider*, *Assign EP to Bed*, *Log Check-In/Check-Out & Deposit Return*.
   - Tab 2: **Arrival & Pickup Logistics Board** with actions: *Assign Buddy*, *Verify 3-Day Guide Sent*, *Mark Pickup Completed*.

## Verification
- Unit test in `tests/accommodation-and-inventory.test.ts` & `tests/arrival-readiness-and-pickup.test.ts` verifying independent schemas and bed allocation constraints.
