# Plan 04-01: Partner Standards per Opportunity & Account Management (M4)

## Objective
Implement the statutory Partner Standards tracking engine (§9.1 Standards #1–#8) tracked per opportunity within account management routines (no per-opportunity survey), enforce meeting logs, reviews, closing criteria, NPS prerequisites validation, and `DQ-062` issue detection.

## User Persona & Role
B2B Partner Relationship Manager (PRM) & Account Manager

## Specialized Skills
`supabase-postgres-best-practices`, `emil-design-eng`, `ui-styling`

## Scope
1. **Migration (`supabase/migrations/20261102000008_icx_partner_standards_v1.sql`)**:
   - Table `partner_standard_check`:
     - `opportunity_id`: uuid not null references opportunity(id) on delete restrict.
     - `std_no`: smallint not null check (std_no between 1 and 8).
     - `delivered`: boolean not null default false.
     - `delivered_at`: timestamptz.
     - `delivered_by`: uuid references member(id) on delete restrict.
     - `meeting_logged_at`: timestamptz.
     - `meeting_notes`: text.
     - `criteria_met`: jsonb not null default '{}'::jsonb.
     - `proof_id`: uuid references proof_document(id) on delete restrict.
     - `metadata`: jsonb not null default '{}'::jsonb.
     - Primary key: (`opportunity_id`, `std_no`).
   - B-tree indexes and triggers for INV-01 and INV-09.
2. **TypeScript Types (`src/lib/types/data-integrity.ts`)**:
   - `PartnerStandardDef`, `PartnerStandardCheck`, `PartnerNpsPrerequisites`.
3. **Partner Standards Service (`src/lib/data-integrity/partner-standards.ts`)**:
   - Statutory definitions of the 8 partner standards (§9.1):
     1. Expectations setting (Meeting logged covering 10 items: LDM, exchange elements, contract/payment, deadlines, timeline, required profile, JD minimums, EP types, comms channel/frequency, visa timeline).
     2. Selection process (Selection mode: partner selects on portal vs AIESEC shortlists).
     3. Job description (Duration, activities/KPIs, skills, working conditions).
     4. Working hours (Within statutory hours limit).
     5. Partner preparation meeting (Goal-setting meeting with EP logged, targets, review date).
     6. Visa & work-permit support (Doc list, deadline agreed, update frequency).
     7. Experience evaluation (Mid review + final review logged; NPS reminder sent).
     8. Partner closing meeting (Held ≤ 1 month after FI, separate from final review, extension/renewal offer).
   - NPS prerequisites checker: raises `DQ-062` warning if partner has no EXPA org / linked employee or portal signup.
   - `computePartnerStandardsDeliveryPct(opportunityId)`: Delivered / 8.
4. **Verification Test Suite (`tests/partner-standards.test.ts`)**:
   - Verification of all 8 standards initialized and tracked per opportunity.
   - Standard 7 validation (requires both mid review and final review logged).
   - Standard 8 closing meeting timing and separation from final review.
   - DQ-062 warning generation when NPS prerequisites are unmet.
   - Prevention of physical hard deletes (INV-01) and audit logging (INV-09).
