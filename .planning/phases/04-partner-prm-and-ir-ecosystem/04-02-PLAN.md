# Plan 04-02: Partner Touchpoint Logging & Buyer Personas (M4)

## Objective
Implement the Partner PRM touchpoint logging engine (§9.2) and buyer-persona profiling system (§5.2, §5.3), supporting all statutory touchpoint types (`monthly_call`, `site_visit`, `lc_touchpoint`, `newsletter`, `quarterly_review`), automatic `last_touchpoint_at` synchronization, persona objections management, and B2B pipeline conversion analytics.

## User Persona & Role
B2B Partner Relationship Manager & Sales Account Executive

## Specialized Skills
`supabase-postgres-best-practices`, `emil-design-eng`, `ui-styling`

## Scope
1. **Migration (`supabase/migrations/20261102000009_icx_partner_touchpoints_v1.sql`)**:
   - Table `partner_touchpoint`:
     - `id`: uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid().
     - `partner_id`: uuid not null references op_partner(id) on delete restrict.
     - `type`: text check in ('monthly_call', 'site_visit', 'lc_touchpoint', 'newsletter', 'quarterly_review').
     - `occurred_at`: timestamptz not null.
     - `actor_id`: uuid not null references member(id) on delete restrict (INV-09).
     - `notes`: text not null.
     - `next_action`: text.
     - `next_action_due_at`: timestamptz.
     - `created_at`: timestamptz not null default now().
     - `deleted_at`, `deleted_reason`: soft delete columns (INV-01).
   - Alter `op_partner` to ensure buyer persona columns exist:
     - `persona`: 'NGO Project Manager' | 'NGO Founder' | 'School Director' | 'Other'.
     - `preferred_channel`: 'call' | 'WhatsApp' | 'email' | 'Facebook/Instagram' | 'meeting'.
     - `objection_tags`: jsonb default '[]'::jsonb.
     - `funnel_stage`: 'lead' | 'called' | 'meeting' | 'open created' | 'contract signed' | 'hosting' | 'retained' | 'lost'.
   - Attach INV-01 and INV-09 triggers.
2. **TypeScript Types (`src/lib/types/data-integrity.ts`)**:
   - `TouchpointType`, `PartnerTouchpoint`, `PartnerPersona`, `PreferredChannel`, `ObjectionTag`, `PartnerFunnelStage`.
3. **Partner Touchpoints Service (`src/lib/data-integrity/partner-touchpoints.ts`)**:
   - `logTouchpoint()`: Records touchpoint and automatically updates `op_partner.last_touchpoint_at`.
   - `updatePartnerPersona()`: Updates persona classification, communication channels, objections, and funnel stage.
   - `computeFunnelConversions()`: Computes Call → Open, Open → APL, APL → APD conversions and process times (§5.3).
   - `softDeleteTouchpoint()`: Enforces INV-01 soft delete with audit trail.
4. **Verification Test Suite (`tests/partner-touchpoints.test.ts`)**:
   - Logging of all 5 touchpoint types.
   - Automatic synchronization of `last_touchpoint_at` (clearing DQ-060 touchpoint gap risk).
   - Validation of buyer personas, preferred channels, and objection tags.
   - Funnel conversion calculations across stages.
   - Soft delete and INV-01 hard delete protection.
