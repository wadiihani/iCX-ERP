# Plan 04-03: IR Partner Record, 2×2 Clustering & E2E Customer Flow Analytics

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M4 — Partner PRM & IR Ecosystem
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §9.3 (IR Partner Record & Clustering S6), §12.1 (One accountable owner INV-03), D6 (IR Cluster Thresholds), D7 (Stage Staleness SLA).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **IR Partner Schema (`ir_partner`)**:
     - Complete 5 lifecycle groups per §9.3:
       - **Define**: `home_entity_id`, `purpose`, `partnership_goals`.
       - **Analyze**: `persona`, `timeline`, `result_history`, `supply` (applicants received), `market_readiness` (available opps).
       - **Promote**: `physical_content`, `digital_content`, `comms_channels`.
       - **Deliver**: `re_growth_target`, `process_time_target_days`, `standards_codelivery_notes`, `strategy`.
       - **Track**: `tracker_status`, `routines`, `review_frequency`, `network_touchpoints`, `investment`, `last_review_at`, `cluster`.
  2. **2×2 Clustering Algorithm**:
     - Four quadrants:
       - `upscale`: High Supply / High Market Readiness ("Innovate together")
       - `grow`: Low Supply / High Market Readiness ("Potential to grow")
       - `understand`: Low Supply / Low Market Readiness ("New cooperation, explore")
       - `invest`: High Supply / Low Market Readiness ("Fix bottlenecks / attraction")
     - Dynamic or benchmark-based threshold calculation (median or configurable threshold D6).
  3. **E2E Customer Flow & Standards Analytics**:
     - Funnel metrics per IR partner: Attraction (Open), Consideration (APL, ACP), Preparation (APD), Experience (RE), Post-Exp (FI, CO).
     - Process speed and conversion rates.
     - E2E standards delivery compliance (% host standards #4–#15 and home standards #1–#3, #16–#17).
     - Identification of stuck EPs in funnel (>14 days per D7).
  4. **System Invariants**:
     - INV-01: Prohibit hard deletes on `ir_partner`.
     - INV-03: Mandatory active owner on `ir_partner`.
     - INV-09: Universal audit logging on all mutations.
