# 07-03-PLAN: Specialist Copilots & Human-in-the-Loop Approval Center UI

## Objective
Implement specialized agent workflows (EP Screening, Partner/IR Intel, Risk Auditor) and complete Svelte 5 frontend views for the AI Copilot Workspace (`/ai`) and the Approval Center (`/approvals`).

## Proposed Changes
1. **Specialist Copilots**:
   - `EP Screening Copilot`: analyzes candidate background, evaluates language and skills match, and creates structured recommendation proposals.
   - `Partner & IR Intel Copilot`: analyzes company engagement and foreign Sending Entity conversion patterns; drafts communication templates.
   - `Standards & Risk Auditor`: audits ongoing exchanges for impending deadlines, missing mandatory evidence, or unassigned accommodations.
   - All consequential mutations routed to the Approval Center as structured proposals.

2. **Frontend Views (`apps/web/`)**:
   - `apps/web/src/lib/api/ai.ts`: client for AI chat, run history, knowledge retrieval, and approvals.
   - `apps/web/src/routes/(app)/approvals/+page.svelte`: Human-in-the-Loop Approval Center:
     - Table/Cards of pending approval proposals.
     - Diff viewer showing Proposed Payload vs Current State.
     - Agent justification and audit provenance.
     - "Approve & Execute" button (triggers backend execution) and "Reject" button (requires reason).
   - `apps/web/src/routes/(app)/ai/+page.svelte`: AI Copilot Workspace:
     - Agent switcher (Policy Assistant, EP Screening, Partner Intel, Risk Auditor).
     - Interactive chat interface with streaming message display.
     - Citation cards with source preview and evidence badges (`[VERIFIED]`, `[OFFICIAL]`).
     - Proposed action preview card with direct "Submit to Approval Queue" button.

3. **Automated Verification**:
   - Pytest tests in `services/api/tests/test_specialist_copilots.py`.
   - SvelteKit `npm run build` verification.
