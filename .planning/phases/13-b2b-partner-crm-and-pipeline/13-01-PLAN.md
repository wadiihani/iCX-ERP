# Plan 13-01: Organization & Deal Relational Split and Slide-Over Edit Drawer

## Objective
Implement Section 3 (`CHG-13`, `CHG-14`) of `aiesec_icx_erp_vercel_audit_and_complete_changes_blueprint.md`: split organizations and deals into two relational collections (`db.organizations` and `db.b2bDeals`), and build a comprehensive Slide-Over Edit Drawer enabling full field updates, contact management, and record archiving.

## Execution Skills
- `vercel-composition-patterns`
- `impeccable`
- `supabase-postgres-best-practices`

## Detailed Tasks
1. Update CRM data models in `src/lib/crm/organizations-data.ts` and `src/lib/types/database.ts`:
   - Split `db.organizations` into:
     - `Organization`: `id`, `legalName`, `brandName`, `legalId` (IF/ICE), `orgType` (`CORPORATE`, `STARTUP`, `NGO`, `EMBASSY_CONSULATE`, `ACADEMIC`), `sector`, `website`, `address`, `city`, `moroccanTaxId`, `healthScore`, `unjustifiedRejectionsCount`, `isArchived`, `createdAt`.
     - `B2bDeal`: `id`, `orgId`, `term`, `peak`, `product` (`iGV`, `iGTa`, `iGTe`, `CSR_SPONSOR`), `stage`, `revenueMad`, `expectedHeadcount`, `assignedOwnerId`, `nextFollowupDate`, `discoveryNotes`, `lossReason`, `contractId`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`.
   - Support `1 Org : N Deals` relation.
2. Build `src/components/crm/partner-edit-drawer.tsx`:
   - Slide-over drawer opening from any Partner card or table row.
   - Tabs:
     - **Profile & Legal**: edit name, brand, Moroccan Tax ID (ICE), sector, website, address.
     - **Active Deals**: list and create multiple deals across terms and products.
     - **Contacts Directory**: inline add, edit, or remove contacts with full role, email, phone, and primary POC toggle.
     - **Danger Zone / Archiving**: soft-archive partner with reason log.

## Verification
- Unit test in `tests/b2b-crm-organizations.test.ts` verifying relational split and update handlers.
