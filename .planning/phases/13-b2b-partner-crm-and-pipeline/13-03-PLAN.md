## Task Specification

### 1. Problem
Currently, partner lists lack a flexible operational view (Kanban vs Table) and partner quality readiness is treated as an opaque or missing score rather than a transparent, evidence-based Partner Standards checklist.

### 2. AIESEC Process & Source Basis
- Exchange Hub SnD learning: Partner Standards measure the quality of partner relationships and ensure purposeful partnerships.
- AEPP 4.2.2.1.7: Unjustified candidate rejection counters must flag partners at risk of sanction.

### 3. Current Implementation Confirmed in Code
- `/sales` renders a hybrid card list.
- Stage updates are simple buttons without multi-view switching or explicit standards checklists.

### 4. Desired Behaviour
1. **3-Way View Switcher on `/sales`**:
   - `[Kanban Board] | [Master CRM Table] | [Detailed Cards]`.
   - Kanban Board features 8 columns with deal value rollups, next action date, and warning borders for stale deals (`>7 days without update`).
   - Master CRM Table features high-density columns, multi-column filtering, and quick export.
2. **OP Candidate Rejection Counter**:
   - Badges: `🟢 0–1 Compliant`, `🟡 2 Warning (1 rejection left)`, `🔴 3+ Suspended (LCVP Intervention per APIP 3.2.8)`.
3. **Partner Standards Register Modal / Drawer**:
   - Checkpoints: Legal Registration Verification, Ethical Fit & AIESEC Values Alignment, Role & Supervision Commitment, Workspace & Health Safeguards, Financial Fee Agreement, Periodic Review / Renewal task.
   - Shows requirement ID, wording, applicability, evidence status, reviewer role, and date.
4. **Context Pre-selection**:
   - Spawning a contact or opportunity from a partner card automatically locks `orgId`.

### 5. Data Model & API Changes
- Add `PartnerStandardCheckpoint`: `id`, `partnerId`, `standardId`, `title`, `applicability`, `evidenceUrl`, `status` (`FULFILLED`, `PENDING`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`), `reviewerId`, `reviewedAt`, `notes`.
- Add view state `viewMode: 'kanban' | 'table' | 'cards'` in `/sales`.

### 6. Permission & Privacy Impact
- Partner Standards checklist can be edited by BD Managers and reviewed by LCVPs.

### 7. Acceptance Tests
- `tests/b2b-deal-pipeline.test.ts`:
  - View switcher rendering and stage column mapping.
  - Rejection counter color coding.
  - Partner standards checklist completion and blocker validation.

### 8. Migration & Rollback Plan
- Non-destructive addition to `/sales` view. Rollback: Default to table view.

### 9. Completion Evidence
- Vitest passing on CRM pipeline suite and verified browser visual render.
