# Plan 05-03: CS Minimums KPI Reporting & Monthly Views (§10.2)

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M5 — Quality Cases & CS Minimums
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §10.2 (KPI view S4), CS Minimum 3 (KPI reporting), CS Minimum 4 (Quality case storage).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **Average Resolution Time**:
     - Formula: mean(`closed_at − opened_at`) in days.
  2. **Open Cases Count**:
     - Count where `closed_at is null`.
  3. **Total Compensation Amount**:
     - Sum by month, grouped by classification (`policy_breach`, `expectation_gap`, `other`) and by type (`refund`, `reimbursement`, `migration_fine`, `goodwill`, `other`).
  4. **Case Recurrence Rate**:
     - Share of cases with `recurrence_of` set (`count(recurrence_of) / total_cases * 100`).
  5. **First-Response Time Metric**:
     - Average elapsed hours between `opened_at` and `first_response_at`.
