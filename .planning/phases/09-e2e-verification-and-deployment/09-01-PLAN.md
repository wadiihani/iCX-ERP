# Plan 09-01: Master E2E Acceptance Test Suite & Realistic Synthetic Fixtures

## Goal
Implement a comprehensive master end-to-end integration test suite verifying all 12 operational journeys defined in the master specification within a unified transaction simulation, and implement a rich synthetic database seeder (`app/seed.py`) providing realistic demonstration data for all operational modules.

## User Requirements Addressed
- All 12 operational journeys from the Master Specification:
  1. PRM Lead Generation & BANT Qualification (score >= 70)
  2. Prohibited Host Rejection (AIESEC entity blocked)
  3. B2B Contract Repository & Capacity Allocation
  4. Deterministic Product Creation (iGV physical, iGTa duration/salary tiers, iGTe accreditation)
  5. IRM Sending Entity Management & Visa Feasibility Citation
  6. EP Candidate Directory, PII Masking & Deduplication
  7. 9-Stage Screening Flow & SLA Clocks (48h First Contact, 72h OP Review)
  8. Acceptance Note Verification & Dual-Status Slot Locking
  9. Exchange Operations, Flight Arrival (CMN/RBA) & Accommodation Bed Assignment
  10. 18 Standards Fulfillment Engine & Deterministic Completion Calculator
  11. Pricing Engine, Payment Obligations, Milestone Tracking & Financial Audit
  12. LangGraph Policy RAG Grounding, Agent Proposal Queuing, and Human Approval Execution
- Rich synthetic database seeder for local demo and testing

  - IRM Sending Entities (Germany, Tunisia, Turkey, Brazil) with SnD analytics
  - EP Candidates with masked PII and unmasked counterparts
  - Applications in various stages (APPLIED, SCREENING, ACCEPTED, APPROVED)
  - Exchanges with airport logistics (CMN, RBA), accommodation units, and bed assignments
  - 18 Standards checklists (with mandatory and optional item fulfillment)
  - Pricing rulesets and payment obligations with partial payments
  - Local policy knowledge documents and chunk embeddings
  - Pending approval proposals and Temporal workflow executions
  - EXPA staging records and discrepancies
