# Plan 03-03: Deadline Engine & Radar Escalations (M3)

## Objective
Implement the statutory Deadline Engine & Radar Escalation system (§8, §13.2) calculating all operational deadlines across exchanges, partners, and national compliance checkpoints, enforcing the Alert Ladder (T-7 notice → T-2 warning → T-0 urgent → overdue), generating statutory DQ issues (DQ-030, DQ-052, DQ-053, DQ-056, DQ-060, DQ-061), and satisfying Acceptance Tests T-12 and T-17.

## User Persona & Role
Operations Manager & Customer Experience Lead

## Specialized Skills
`supabase-postgres-best-practices`, `emil-design-eng`, `ui-styling`

## Scope
1. **Migration (`supabase/migrations/20261102000007_icx_deadline_radar_v1.sql`)**:
   - Schema structure for deadline tracking and radar aggregation.
2. **Deadline Engine Core (`src/lib/data-integrity/deadline-engine.ts`)**:
   - `computeExchangeDeadlines()`:
     - Visa document delivery: `approved_at + 14 d` (§8, S9). If day 15+ without visa proof, raises urgent `DQ-053` (Acceptance Test T-12).
     - Departure support procedure: `fi_date - 14 d` (§8, S1). If standard #15 untracked at cutoff, raises `DQ-056` warning.
     - APD past RE date: flags urgent `DQ-052` on day 1+, enables direct break on day 21+ for GV.
     - Stage staleness: active records with no update for > 14 d raise `DQ-030` warning.
   - `computePartnerDeadlines()`:
     - Touchpoint gap: `last_touchpoint_at + 35 d`. If >= 36 days without contact, raises `DQ-060` warning (Acceptance Test T-17).
     - Partner closing meeting: `fi_date + 30 d`. If past 30 days after FI, raises `DQ-061`.
   - `computeComplianceRadar()`:
     - ECB quarterly audit report deadlines (Apr 28, Jul 28, Oct 28, Jan 28).
     - ICB Audit Check cycles (March, September).
   - **Alert Ladder & Escalation Hierarchy (§8)**:
     - T-7 notice → T-2 warning (escalates to LCVP) → T-0 urgent (escalates to LCVP) → overdue (daily escalation to LCP).
3. **Acceptance Test Suite (`tests/deadline-engine.test.ts`)**:
   - **T-12**: Approval on day 0 with visa required and no visa docs on day 15 raises urgent DQ-053.
   - **T-17**: Partner with no touchpoint for 36 days raises DQ-060 warning.
   - Departure support SLA (fi_date - 14 d) and DQ-056 warning.
   - Alert ladder status transitions and escalation levels (owner → LCVP → LCP).
   - Compliance radar deadlines (ECB quarterly and ICB audit cycles).
