# Plan 07-01: Data Quality Rules Suite (DQ-001..DQ-084) & Scanner Engine

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M7 — Data Quality Engine & Integrity Console
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §13.2 (Data-quality rules), §14 (Open DQ issues).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **Comprehensive 26 Statutory DQ Rules Suite**:
     - **Identity & Uniqueness**:
       - `DQ-001`: Duplicate `expa_ep_id` (Blocker)
       - `DQ-002`: Fuzzy duplicate EP (normalised name + home entity) (Warn)
       - `DQ-003`: Fuzzy duplicate partner (normalised name + city / email domain) (Warn)
     - **Accountability & Ownership**:
       - `DQ-010`: Record without owner (Blocker)
       - `DQ-011`: Owner is inactive member (Blocker -> handoff task)
     - **Integrity & Completeness**:
       - `DQ-020`: Required fields missing for current stage (Warn / Blocker at transition)
       - `DQ-021`: Exchange without opportunity, or opp without partner (Blocker)
       - `DQ-030`: Record not updated within stage SLA (default 14d) (Warn)
       - `DQ-031`: Last EXPA sync older than `max_age_hours` (Blocker)
     - **Reconciliation & Synchronization**:
       - `DQ-040`: Tracker status != EXPA status (Blocker)
       - `DQ-041`: Date mismatch (approved / RE / FI / CO) (Warn)
       - `DQ-042`: EP in EXPA missing from tracker / reverse (Blocker / Warn)
     - **Standards & Programme Compliance**:
       - `DQ-050`: Duration outside current rule version (Warn)
       - `DQ-051`: Weekly hours outside current rule version (Warn)
       - `DQ-052`: APD with RE date passed (Urgent)
       - `DQ-053`: Visa docs not delivered within 14d of approval (Urgent)
       - `DQ-054`: Mandatory host standard untracked after approval (Warn)
       - `DQ-055`: Tracked vs EP-reported standard mismatch (Review)
       - `DQ-056`: Departure support not sent by `fi_date - 14d` (Warn)
     - **Governance & Requests**:
       - `DQ-057`: Status request submitted with incomplete checklist or past cutoff (Blocker)
       - `DQ-058`: Request covers more than one EP (Blocker)
     - **Partnerships & PRM**:
       - `DQ-060`: Partner without touchpoint within `touchpoint_max_gap_days` (35d) (Warn)
       - `DQ-061`: Partner closing meeting not held <= 1 month after FI (Warn)
       - `DQ-062`: Partner not linked for NPS (Warn)
     - **Quality Cases & Invariants**:
       - `DQ-070`: Case closed with any of 5 mandatory fields missing (Blocker)
       - `DQ-071`: Compensation > 0 without financial proof (Blocker)
       - `DQ-080`: `exchange.status` != last `status_history.to_status` (Blocker)
       - `DQ-081`: Hard delete attempted (Blocker)
       - `DQ-082`: Import row drop > `max_row_drop_pct` (Blocker)
       - `DQ-083`: Proof link dead or checksum changed (Warn)
       - `DQ-084`: PII pattern (phone/email/national ID) in free-text notes (Warn)
  2. **Issue Lifecycle & SLA Engine**:
     - SLA deadlines by severity:
       - Urgent: 1 day (24h)
       - Blocker: 2 days (48h)
       - Warn: 7 days
       - Review: 7 days
     - Automated scan execution (`runNightlyDataQualityScan`).
     - Auto-resolution when condition clears, logged in `audit_log`.
