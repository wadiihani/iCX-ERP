# Plan 07-02: Weighted Data Health Score Algorithm (§14)

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M7 — Data Quality Engine & Integrity Console
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §14 (Data Health Score, "Are we safe?").
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **Four Weighted Dimensions**:
     - **Completeness (35% weight)**: % of required fields populated across active customer flow stages (§5.1 matrix).
     - **Freshness & Timeliness (25% weight)**: % of records compliant with stage SLAs (14d idle limit) and partner touchpoint gap SLA (35d limit).
     - **EXPA Consistency (25% weight)**: % of records in sync with EXPA without status discrepancies (DQ-040) or date drifts (DQ-041).
     - **Ownership & Accountability (15% weight)**: % of records with active and assigned owners (zero orphans DQ-010/DQ-011).
  2. **Score Output & Grading**:
     - Aggregate 0–100 integer score.
     - Grade classification:
       - 90–100: **A (Optimal Integrity)**
       - 80–89: **B (Healthy / Minor Warnings)**
       - 70–79: **C (At Risk / Needs Attention)**
       - 60–69: **D (Degraded / Urgent Fixes Needed)**
       - < 60: **F (Critical Compromise / Immediate Action Required)**
     - Sub-score breakdown with drill-down metrics.
