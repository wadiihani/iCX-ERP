<objective>
Implement the B2B Partner Relationship Management (PRM) lead pipeline, BANT qualification engine, call/meeting logs, and automated follow-up tasks.

Purpose: Enables the local Business Development (BD) team to systematically identify, qualify, contact, negotiate, and convert hosting partners.
</objective>

<tasks>
1. Define Pydantic schemas for leads, stage transitions, BANT qualification assessment, and interaction activities.
2. Implement deterministic BANT scoring logic: weighted scores across Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline with clear qualification thresholds.
3. Build service methods for lead lifecycle: stage transitions, activity logs, automated next-step task creation, and lead aging metrics.
4. Implement REST router at `/api/v1/prm` with endpoints for lead CRUD, stage update, activity logging, and pipeline summary.
5. Author automated tests in `services/api/tests/test_prm_pipeline.py` verifying stage transitions, BANT scoring, and activity logging.
</tasks>
