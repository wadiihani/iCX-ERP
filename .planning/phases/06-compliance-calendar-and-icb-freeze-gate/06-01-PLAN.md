# Plan 06-01: Compliance Item Schema & 13 Statutory ICB Documents

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M6 — Compliance Calendar & ICB Freeze Gate
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §11 (Module F — Compliance calendar, S3, S9), APIP Annex 4, §12.1 (INV-03).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **Compliance Item Schema (`compliance_item`)**:
     - `id`: UUID PK
     - `kind`: enum (`icb_audit_doc`, `ecb_quarterly_report`, `unfreezing_submission`, `internal_audit`)
     - `doc_number`: integer (1–13 for ICB audit documents)
     - `title`: text
     - `section`: text (General, 3.1.1 Legal Support, 3.1.2 Risk Management, 3.1.3 Educational, 3.1.4 Quality Management)
     - `due_at`: timestamptz
     - `submitted_at`: timestamptz
     - `result`: enum (`passed`, `failed`, `pending`, `waived`)
     - `pass_pct`: numeric
     - `evidence_ids`: array of proof/evidence IDs
     - `violation_level`: smallint (1–5 where known)
     - `notes`: text
     - `owner_id`: UUID FK -> `member(id)` (mandatory active owner INV-03)
  2. **13 Statutory ICB Audit Documents Catalogue (§11.2, APIP Annex 4)**:
     - 1. Programmes statement (General)
     - 2. Lawyer / PR establishment letter (3.1.1)
     - 3. Contract explanation (3.1.1)
     - 4. PHAVC establishment letter (3.1.2)
     - 5. ECB establishment letter (3.1.2)
     - 6. Contacts updated in ECB global contact list (3.1.2)
     - 7. ESC establishment letter (3.1.2)
     - 8. Risk management plan (3.1.2)
     - 9. Email template of health & safety measures (3.1.2)
     - 10. Education plan (L4 + L3) (3.1.3)
     - 11. SOPs updated to external national context (3.1.3)
     - 12. Management tool for quality cases (3.1.4)
     - 13. ECB audit system (3.1.4)
  3. **Seed & Verification Service (`complianceService`)**:
     - Automatically seed or check 13 statutory documents for the LC.
     - Submission, review, and evidence tracking.
     - Universal audit log (INV-09), prohibition of hard delete (INV-01).
