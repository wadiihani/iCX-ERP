# Plan 08-02: Handoff Workflow & Packet Generator (T-15)

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M8 — Ownership Handoff & Disaster Recovery
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §12.2 (Handoff packet), §12.3 (Handoff workflow), §16 (Acceptance Test T-15).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **10-Section Handoff Packet**:
     - 1. Open exchanges grouped by stage with next deadline
     - 2. Overdue / due in 14 days from deadline engine
     - 3. Open requests (breaks / FI / extensions)
     - 4. Open cases with compensation exposure
     - 5. Partners (stage, last touchpoint, next action, open standards)
     - 6. IR partners (cluster, last review)
     - 7. Open DQ issues by severity
     - 8. Proof index (all linked documents with checksum status)
     - 9. Change log (last 30 days from `audit_log`)
     - 10. Access list (roles and who has them)
  2. **Handoff FSM Workflow**:
     - `initiated -> packet_generated -> reviewed_by_outgoing -> acknowledged_by_incoming -> transferred -> 7_day_watch`
  3. **Acceptance Test T-15 Enforcement**:
     - "Given a handoff, when incoming owner hasn't acknowledged, then ownership does not transfer."
     - Bulk reassignment executes ONLY after incoming owner acknowledges.
