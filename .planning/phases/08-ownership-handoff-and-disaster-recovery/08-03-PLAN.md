# Plan 08-03: Disaster Recovery, Snapshot Manifest & Restore Verifier (T-14)

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M8 — Ownership Handoff & Disaster Recovery
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §13.3 (Backup and export), §16 (Acceptance Test T-14).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **Nightly Snapshot Engine**:
     - Exports all operational tables to CSV/JSON format.
     - Computes SHA-256 hash per table dataset.
     - Generates manifest with table name, row count, sha256 checksum, and timestamp.
     - Retention schedule: 30 daily + 12 weekly snapshots.
  2. **Acceptance Test T-14 (Scripted Restore & Verification)**:
     - "Given last night's snapshot, when restored to a scratch DB, then row counts and checksums match the manifest."
     - Verifies exact checksum matching and zero row loss upon restore.

- Tests in `tests/handoff-and-disaster-recovery.test.ts`.
