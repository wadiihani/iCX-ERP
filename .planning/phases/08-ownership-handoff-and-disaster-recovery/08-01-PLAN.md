# Plan 08-01: Accountable Ownership & RACI Synergy Interfaces

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Phase**: M8 — Ownership Handoff & Disaster Recovery
- **Spec Reference**: `iCX_Data_Integrity_Tracking_Spec.md` §12.1 (Ownership model, G2, G5), S8 ("never decide without understanding your RACI"), §13.2 (DQ-010, DQ-011).
- **Core Requirements**:
  1. **One Accountable Owner per Record**:
     - Mandatory `owner_id` enforced on `op_partner`, `opportunity`, `exchange`, `case_quality`, `status_request`, `ir_partner`, `compliance_item` (INV-03).
  2. **RACI Matrix Support**:
     - Optional `raci` object: `{ responsible: string[]; accountable: string; consulted: string[]; informed: string[] }`.
  3. **Synergy Interfaces**:
     - Functional synergy tags: `marketing`, `b2b`, `tm_mxp`, `finance_legal`.
  4. **Orphan & Inactive Owner Detection**:
     - When owner is deactivated (DQ-011), auto-generate a handoff / reassignment task to their manager.
