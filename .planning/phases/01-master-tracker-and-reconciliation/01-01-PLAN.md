# Plan 01-01: Master Tracker Relational CRUD Data Layer & Services (M1)

## Objective
Implement strongly typed, production-grade operational data services and repository abstractions for the five core Master Tracker entities: `OpPartner`, `Opportunity`, `Contract`, `Ep`, and `Exchange`. All mutations must enforce the non-negotiable integrity invariants (INV-01 soft-deletes only, INV-03 mandatory active owner, INV-09 audit logging, INV-10 confidentiality, and DQ-080 synchronized status history).

## User Persona & Role
Full-Stack Engineer & Database Architect

## Specialized Skills
`supabase-postgres-best-practices`, `xlsx`, `data-table-engine`

## Scope
1. **`OpPartner` Service**:
   - Create, Read, Update, Soft-delete with actor attribution.
   - Buyer-persona tags, preferred communication channel, objection tags (§5.2).
   - Last touchpoint tracking and stage progression.
2. **`Opportunity` Service**:
   - Create, Read, Update, Soft-delete.
   - Slot capacity computation (total, filled, available capacity).
   - Rule-based validation of `duration_weeks` and `hours_per_week` via `src/lib/data-integrity/rules.ts` (INV-08).
3. **`Contract` Service**:
   - Create, Read, Update, Soft-delete.
   - Payment terms, structured key deadlines, document storage link.
4. **`Ep` Service**:
   - Create, Read, Update, Soft-delete.
   - Enforce uniqueness of `expa_ep_id` (DQ-001).
   - Enforce confidentiality masking of contact fields based on viewer role and ownership (INV-10, T-18).
5. **`Exchange` Service**:
   - Create exchange record ensuring `UNIQUE (ep_id, opportunity_id)`.
   - Automatically instantiate 16 `standard_check` records (#1–#16) in `not_started` status (§5.1).
   - Atomic status transition with synchronized `status_history` ledger entry (DQ-080).
   - Soft-delete with mandatory actor and reason (INV-01).

## Verification
- Comprehensive test suite in `tests/master-tracker-crud.test.ts` verifying:
  - CRUD operations across all 5 entities.
  - Automatic `standard_check` (#1–#16) initialization upon exchange creation.
  - Rejection of physical hard deletes (INV-01).
  - Status transition synchronization into `status_history` (DQ-080).
  - EP contact data masking for unauthorized viewers (INV-10).
