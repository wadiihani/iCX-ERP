Implement Section 10 (`CHG-40`) of `aiesec_icx_erp_vercel_audit_and_complete_changes_blueprint.md`: establish the dedicated `/members` module for AIESEC in Rabat with member roster, Composite Member Engagement Score (`MES 0–100%`), and automatic 4-tier classification.

## Detailed Tasks
1. Create `src/lib/members/member-engagement-types.ts` & `src/lib/members/member-data.ts`:
   - Member model: `id`, `fullName`, `email`, `whatsappPhone`, `role` (`LCVP`, `MM_TL`, `TM`), `subDepartment` (`EXTERNAL_BD`, `INTERNAL_OPS`, `CXP_QUALITY`, `FINANCE`), `attendanceScore`, `quotaScore`, `slaScore`, `o2oScore`, `compositeMes`, `tier`.
   - Preload AIESEC in Rabat roster: *Wadii Hani, Omar Joukhane, Mouatassim Benkhallouq, Rayen Jbali, Firdawsse Jray, Abdenasser Moussafi, Nourdine Ettaoumi, Mohamed El Kahri, Salma Sahl*, etc.
   - Composite MES formula implementation:
     `MES = (0.35 * Attendance) + (0.30 * QuotaAttainment) + (0.20 * SlaOnTime) + (0.15 * O2OParticipation)`
   - 4-Tier classification logic:
     - `🟢 Tier 1: Core Engaged Group (90%–100%)`
     - `🔵 Tier 2: Strong Engagement (70%–89%)`
     - `🟡 Tier 3: Moderate / Partial Engagement (20%–69%) — Flag for TL O2O`
     - `🔴 Tier 4: Critical Drop-Off Risk (<20%) — Flag for LCVP + TM Intervention`
2. Build `src/app/members/page.tsx`:
   - Top metrics bar: Total Members, Tier 1 Core Count, Tier 4 Risk Count, Avg LC Engagement %, O2O Completion %.
   - Interactive Member Roster Table: search, filter by Department/Tier, sort by MES score.
   - Tier distribution visual bar and quick filter pills.
