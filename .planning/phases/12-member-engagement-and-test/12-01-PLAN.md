## Task Specification

### 1. Problem
Currently, member information is partially conflated with finance or admin tabs without a dedicated `/members` operating module. Team leaders cannot assess real-time engagement or identify drop-off risk members before they disengage, jeopardizing exchange delivery.

### 2. AIESEC Process & Source Basis
From the AIESEC Hub (The AIESEC Way & Membership/Functional Hubs):
- Membership is a leadership development vehicle requiring active engagement tracking, transparent expectations, and support.
- AIESEC in Rabat mandate 26.2 operational routines require a multi-factor engagement measurement covering meeting attendance, quota achievement, operational SLA punctuality, and 1:1 coaching.

### 3. Current Implementation Confirmed in Code
- `src/lib/types/` has basic user interfaces.
- Navigation already has `/admin/roles-and-access` and `/dashboards/role-view`, but no dedicated `/members` directory page.
- Left sidebar already includes `11. Member Engagement & TeSt` linking to `/members`.

### 4. Desired Behaviour
1. Navigate to `/members` to view the comprehensive AIESEC in Rabat member roster.
2. Calculate Composite Member Engagement Score (`MES 0–100%`) using the weighted formula:
   $$\text{MES} = (0.35 \times \text{Attendance}) + (0.30 \times \text{QuotaAttainment}) + (0.20 \times \text{SlaOnTime}) + (0.15 \times \text{O2OParticipation})$$
3. Categorize each member automatically into 4 engagement tiers:
   - `🟢 Tier 1: Core Engaged Group (90%–100%)`
   - `🔵 Tier 2: Strong Engagement (70%–89%)`
   - `🟡 Tier 3: Moderate / Partial Engagement (20%–69%) — Flag for TL O2O`
   - `🔴 Tier 4: Critical Drop-Off Risk (<20%) — Flag for LCVP + TM Intervention`
4. Top headline scorecard displaying Total Members, Tier 1 Core, Tier 4 Risk, Average LC Engagement %, and O2O Completion %.
5. Interactive search, department filter (`External Engine BD`, `Internal Engine Ops`, `FnL / TM`), tier filter, and sortable columns.

### 5. Data Model & API Changes
- Create `src/lib/members/member-engagement-types.ts`:
  - `MemberRole`: `'LCVP' | 'MM_TL' | 'TM'`
  - `MemberDepartment`: `'EXTERNAL_BD' | 'INTERNAL_OPS' | 'CXP_QUALITY' | 'FINANCE'`
  - `MemberRecord`: `id`, `fullName`, `email`, `whatsappPhone`, `role`, `department`, `attendanceScore`, `quotaScore`, `slaScore`, `o2oScore`, `compositeMes`, `tier`, `assignedEpsCount`, `assignedDealsCount`.
- Create `src/lib/members/member-data.ts`:
  - Preloaded roster for LC Rabat Mandate 26.2.
  - Pure calculation functions `calculateCompositeMes(scores)` and `deriveEngagementTier(mes)`.

### 6. Permission & Privacy Impact
- Role-based visibility: Members can view their own score and general roster; Team Leaders and LCVPs have drill-down coaching access.
- Sensitive personal notes restricted to coaches and LCVPs.

### 7. Acceptance Tests
- `tests/member-engagement.test.ts`:
  - Validates MES weighted formula calculation across edge cases (0%, 100%, mixed).
  - Validates correct tier assignment at threshold boundaries (19%, 20%, 69%, 70%, 89%, 90%).
  - Validates filtering by department and search by name.

### 8. Migration & Rollback Plan
- Pure client-safe data engine with backward-compatible types; no destructive database alterations.
- Rollback: Revert `/members` route to redirect to `/admin/roles-and-access`.

### 9. Completion Evidence
- Screenshot or render markup showing `/members` table with active tier badges.
- Vitest test report passing all test assertions with zero failures.
