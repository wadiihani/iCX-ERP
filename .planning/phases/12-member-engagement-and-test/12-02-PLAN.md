# Plan 12-02: Rabat 6 Governance Routines Attendance Heatmap, O2O Logger & TeSt Scorecard

## Objective
Implement Section 10 (`CHG-41`, `CHG-42`) of `aiesec_icx_erp_vercel_audit_and_complete_changes_blueprint.md`: build interactive trackers for Rabat's 6 mandatory governance routines with GitHub-style weekly attendance heatmap, a monthly O2O coaching logger drawer, and the AIESEC Team Standards (`TeSt`) scorecard.

## Execution Skills
- `impeccable`
- `ui-styling`
- `ui-ux-pro-max`
- `animate`

## Detailed Tasks
1. Build `src/components/members/governance-attendance-grid.tsx`:
   - Tracks 6 mandatory governance spaces from Rabat operational models:
     - `Weekly Team Meeting`
     - `Weekly Core Meeting`
     - `Bi-Monthly Dept Meeting`
     - `Monthly LCM`
     - `Monthly O2O`
     - `Monthly LCP Calibration`
   - Attendance status encoding: On time (`≤5m`), Late (`>15m`), Absent, Excused.
   - GitHub-style weekly attendance & O2O (`⭐`) heatmap component across weeks `W1` through `W16`.
2. Build `src/components/members/o2o-coaching-modal.tsx`:
   - Triggered from member row action (`Log O2O Session`).
   - Fields: `O2O Date`, `Coach` (`TL` or `LCVP`), `Coachee` (`TM` or `TL`), `Wellbeing / Motivation Score (1–5)`, `Personal KPI Review`, `Identified Blockers`, `Agreed Action Items`, `Follow-up Date`.
   - Updates member's `o2oScore` and recalculates MES reactively.
3. Build `src/components/members/team-standards-scorecard.tsx`:
   - Interactive scorecard based on AIESEC Global Team Standards:
     - **Stage 1: Building** (`Team Creation & Rules`, `Team Plan & Individual Quotas`, `JD & Personal Development Plan [PDP]`)
     - **Stage 2: Performing** (`Weekly Team Meetings`, `Individual KPI Accountability`, `Monthly O2Os`, `Recognition & Incentives`, `Feedback Spaces`)
     - **Stage 3: Closing** (`Team Performance Debrief`, `PDP Debrief & Leadership Transition`, `100% ERP Stakeholder & Knowledge Handover`)
   - Computes overall TeSt completion percentage per team leader (`MM External` & `MM Internal`).

## Verification
- Unit test in `tests/team-standards-and-governance.test.ts` verifying attendance aggregation, O2O score updates, and TeSt percentage calculation.
