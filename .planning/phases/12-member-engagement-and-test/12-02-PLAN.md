## Task Specification

### 1. Problem
Attendance tracking and 1:1 coaching currently occur in disparate chat groups or unlinked sheets. Team Leaders lack a structured way to assess Global Team Standards (`Building`, `Performing`, `Closing`) and at mandate turnover, orphaned accounts lead to lost partner context and dropped EP communications.

### 2. AIESEC Process & Source Basis
- AIESEC Global Team Standards (`TeSt`): Building (Team Rules, Plan, Quotas, PDP), Performing (Meetings, Accountability, O2Os, Recognition), Closing (Performance Debrief, Leadership Transition, Handover).
- AIESEC in Rabat 6 Mandatory Governance Routines: Weekly Team Meeting, Weekly Core Meeting, Bi-Monthly Dept Meeting, Monthly LCM, Monthly O2O, Monthly LCP Calibration.
- Mandate Transition Protocol: Structured Handover Pack compiling active accounts, deals, EPs, tasks, open risks, and financial claims.

### 3. Current Implementation Confirmed in Code
- `/admin` has basic workload and handoff modals.
- No integrated attendance heatmap or TeSt scorecard exists.

### 4. Desired Behaviour
1. **Rabat 6 Governance Attendance Heatmap**:
   - Visual grid tracking attendance across Weeks `W1` through `W16`.
   - Distinct statuses: On time (`≤5m`, green), Late (`>15m`, amber), Absent (red), Excused (slate), O2O Completed (`⭐`).
2. **Monthly O2O Coaching Logger**:
   - Slide-over / modal drawer to log 1:1 coaching sessions.
   - Captures: Date, Coach, Coachee, Wellbeing Score (1-5), Personal KPI review, Blockers, Agreed Actions, Follow-up date.
   - Reactively increments `o2oScore` and recalculates member MES.
3. **Team Standards (TeSt) Scorecard**:
   - Interactive checklist for Building (3 items), Performing (5 items), and Closing (3 items).
   - Real-time TeSt completion percentage indicator for Team Leaders.
4. **Mandate Handover Pack Generator**:
   - Exportable structured handover summary: active partners, deals, contracts, IR quotas, active EPs, overdue tasks, pending financial claims, and risk escalations.

### 5. Data Model & API Changes
- Extend `src/lib/members/member-engagement-types.ts` with:

  - `O2OSessionRecord`: `id`, `memberId`, `coachId`, `date`, `wellbeingScore`, `kpiReviewNotes`, `blockers`, `actionItems`, `followUpDate`.
  - `TeamStandardsState`: `building: Record<string, boolean>`, `performing: Record<string, boolean>`, `closing: Record<string, boolean>`.
