# Plan 15-01: Atomic Typed EP Fields, Relational Foreign Keys & Searchable Visa Regime Engine

## Objective
Implement Section 5 (`CHG-25`, `CHG-26`) of `aiesec_icx_erp_vercel_audit_and_complete_changes_blueprint.md`: decompose concatenated EP text inputs into atomic typed fields, establish relational foreign keys to accommodations and team members, and implement the searchable nationality and Morocco visa regime resolver.

## Execution Skills
- `supabase-postgres-best-practices`
- `impeccable`
- `ui-styling`

## Detailed Tasks
1. Refactor EP Schema in `src/lib/types/database.ts` & `src/lib/ep/ep-data.ts`:
   - Replace 6 concatenated strings with typed atomic fields:
     - `fullName` (`TEXT`) + `expaPersonId` (`NUMBER`) + `dob` (`DATE`, validate age `18..30`)
     - `email` (`EMAIL`) + `whatsappPhone` (`TEL`)
     - `passportNumber` (`TEXT`) + `passportExpiryDate` (`DATE`, validate `>= slotEnd + 180 days`)
     - `sendingIrId` (`FOREIGN KEY` to `db.irPartners`) + `sePocName` + `sePocPhone`
     - `arrivalDate` (`DATE`) + `arrivalTime` (`TIME`) + `arrivalPort` (`SELECT: CMN / RBA / Rabat Agdal / Rabat Ville / Tangier`) + `flightNumber` (`TEXT`)
     - `accommProviderId` & `bedId` (`FOREIGN KEY` to vetted `db.accommodations`) + `pickupBuddyUserId` & `epManagerUserId` (`FOREIGN KEY` to `db.teamMembers`).
2. Build Morocco Visa Regime Resolver in `src/lib/ir/moroccan-visa-matrix.ts`:
   - Inputs: `nationalityCountry` (searchable country list) + `residenceCountry` (for EU/US/UK residence permit AEVM eligibility).
   - Resolves to:
     - `VISA_FREE_90D` (14 days minimum lead time)
     - `AEVM_EVISA` (21 days minimum lead time)
     - `CONSULAR_EMBASSY_VISA` (45 days minimum lead time)
   - Emits lead time warning if `daysUntilRealization < requiredVisaLeadDays`.
3. Update EP creation & edit drawer in `src/components/funnel/ep-edit-drawer.tsx` with structured atomic inputs.
