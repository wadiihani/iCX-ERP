## Task Specification

### 1. Problem
Financial records are sometimes conflated with team roster items or treated as simple text memos. Single-member payouts risk non-compliance with AFPM rules, and contracted revenue is frequently confused with confirmed cash received.

### 2. AIESEC Process & Source Basis
- AIESEC Finance & Sustainability Hub / AFPM guidance:
  - Strict separation of Budget, Expected Revenue, Invoices/Receivables, Confirmed Inflows, Expenses/Payables, Cash Outflows, Refunds, and Reconciliations.
  - Dual-approval requirement on disbursements and payments.
  - Mandatory receipt attachment and strict invoice aging warnings (>14 days overdue).

### 3. Current Implementation Confirmed in Code
- `/dashboards` and `/dashboards/role-view` render finance tabs.
- Some demo values exist; full ledger separation and dual-approval state machine need strict enforcement.

### 4. Desired Behaviour
1. **Complete Ledger Separation**:
   - `db.transactions` records: amount, currency (MAD), classification, linked partner/opportunity/EP, receipt URL, creator, approver, status.
   - Prevent treating contracted or expected revenue as cash received.
2. **Dual-Approval State Machine (`SM-08`)**:
   - Payouts require approval from both Finance Manager and LCVP before transition to `PAID`.
   - Rejection requires mandatory explanation.
3. **Invoices & Accounts Receivable Table (`db.invoices`)**:
   - Tracks Invoice Number, Partner/Sponsor/EP, Product, Total TTC MAD, Paid MAD, Balance Due MAD, Issue Date, Due Date.
   - Prominent badge for `>14 DAYS OVERDUE` triggering partner freeze alerts per AFPM Section 6 and APIP 3.2.5.
4. **Annex 2 Financial Provision Link**:
   - When a Quality Case calculates compensation/liability in MAD, a provision transaction is automatically created in the AFPM ledger.

### 5. Data Model & API Changes
- Standardize `Transaction`: `id`, `category` (`INFLOW_PARTNER_FEE`, `INFLOW_EP_DEPOSIT`, `INFLOW_SPONSOR`, `OUTFLOW_HOUSING`, `OUTFLOW_PICKUP`, `OUTFLOW_REFUND`, `OUTFLOW_ADMIN`), `amountMad`, `currency`, `status` (`DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL_1`, `PENDING_APPROVAL_2`, `APPROVED`, `PAID`, `REJECTED`), `receiptUrl`, `linkedOrgId`, `linkedEpId`, `linkedCaseId`, `approver1Id`, `approver2Id`.
- Standardize `Invoice`: `id`, `invoiceNumber`, `orgId`, `dealId`, `totalMad`, `paidMad`, `balanceMad`, `dueDate`, `status` (`PAID`, `PARTIAL`, `UNPAID`, `OVERDUE`).

### 6. Permission & Privacy Impact
- Financial records accessible only to Finance Role, VP FnL, and LCP.

  - Annex 2 compensation provision creation.
